## 7. How to sign

**Individuals:** in your first pull request, add this comment:

```text
I have read the CDK Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md, version 1.0) and I hereby sign it.
```

Commits must also carry a `Signed-off-by:` line (`git commit -s`), which
certifies the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/)
for each commit.

**Organizations:** contact the maintainers through a GitHub issue titled
"Entity CLA request" (without confidential details) to arrange an entity
agreement covering your employees.
